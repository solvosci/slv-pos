Extends the Point of Sale closing process by allowing cash outs from the
cash register at session closing.

It supports two behaviors depending on the POS configuration:

- Manual cash out: the user can enter the amount to be cashed out during
  the closing popup.
- Automatic cash out: any cash exceeding the opening balance is
  automatically cashed out when the session is closed.

All cash outs are recorded using the standard POS cash in/out mechanism.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

The session opening cash amount is used as the reference both for
automatic cash out and for the suggested cash out amount in manual mode,
in order to always keep consistency between session openings. However,
POS allows performing initial cash adjustments after the session has
been opened. The goal is that the initial cash reference should be
updated to take the initial adjustment into account. The issue arises
because Odoo does not distinguish between a manual cash in/out operation
and an initial cash adjustment.
