The session opening cash amount is used as the reference both for automatic cash out and for the suggested cash out amount in manual mode, in order to always keep consistency between session openings. However, POS allows performing initial cash adjustments after the session has been opened. The goal is that the initial cash reference should be updated to take the initial adjustment into account. The issue arises because Odoo does not distinguish between a manual cash in/out operation and an initial cash adjustment.
