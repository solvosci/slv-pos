Extends the Point of Sale closing process by allowing cash
outs from the cash register at session closing.

It supports two behaviors depending on the POS configuration:
- Manual cash out: the user can enter the amount to be cashed out during the closing popup.
- Automatic cash out: any cash exceeding the opening balance is automatically cashed out when the session is closed.

All cash outs are recorded using the standard POS cash in/out
mechanism.
